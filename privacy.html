Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Last Updated: October 2026

1. Overview & Commitment

At Mehvi (accessible at https://mehvireadtime.netlify.app/), we believe that utility tools should respect user privacy by default. This Privacy Policy details our operational practices regarding the handling of information when you use the Read Time Calculator for Articles & Blogs.

2. In-Browser Client-Side Text Processing

The most important privacy feature of Mehvi is our zero-transmission architecture:

  • No Server Uploads: When you paste, write, or analyze text in our calculator, all word counting, sentence parsing, character counts, and reading-time calculations are executed strictly within your local browser memory using client-side JavaScript.
  • No Data Storage: We do not log, transmit, intercept, or store your text, article drafts, manuscripts, or essays on any web server, cloud database, or third-party API.
  • Transient Memory: Your text exists solely in your browser's temporary working session. When you refresh, navigate away, or close your browser tab, the text is instantly cleared from memory.

3. Information We Collect

Because our core calculator is purely client-side, we do not require account registration, email sign-ups, or personal identity verification:

  • Personal Data: We do not collect names, addresses, or phone numbers unless you choose to voluntarily submit an inquiry through our contact form.
  • Standard Server Logs: Like standard web hosts, our hosting servers may automatically record standard anonymous request logs (IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, and request timestamps) strictly for infrastructure security, DDoS mitigation, and server maintenance.
