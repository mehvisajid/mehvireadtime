Core Purpose

Our Mission & Engineering Vision

Why we built Mehavi ReadTime and how we approach privacy, performance, and accessibility.

Our Mission

Speed, precision & barrier-free utility

To provide fast, accurate, and completely free web utility tools that help users calculate exact reading and speaking duration for any text instantly.

We believe pacing shouldn't require complex software or guesswork. Whether you are rehearsing a critical 5-minute keynote presentation, estimating reading time for a technical blog post, or pacing study scripts, Mehavi ReadTime delivers immediate calculations directly in your browser.

  • Instantaneous Execution: Zero page reloads or waiting loops.
  • Research-Grounded: Calibrated with standard cognitive reading speeds.
  • Always 100% Free: No paywalls, no tiered subscriptions, no gimmicks.

Why Choose Us

Privacy-first engineering by design

Unlike conventional online counting utilities that transmit your proprietary drafts, essays, or personal speeches to remote servers, Mehavi ReadTime operates with uncompromising security.

  • 100% Client-Side Privacy: No text ever leaves your machine. Calculations execute entirely in local browser RAM.
  • Zero Registration Required: No email signups, no passwords to create, no verification codes. Just open and use.
  • Superfast Instant Calculations: Responsive feedback with zero processing overhead.
  • Clean Ad-Free Focus: Clean interface engineered for deep work without popups or clutter.
