How Many Words is a 10 Minute YouTube Video?

Quick Answer: A standard 10-minute YouTube video script requires between **1,300 to 1,500 words** when speaking at a normal, engaging conversational pace of 130 to 150 words per minute.

If you are planning video content, getting the script length right is essential. Writing too much means you will have to cut material or speak rushed, while writing too little leaves awkward pauses. Much like preparing for a presentation or checking a 5-minute speech script, knowing your word count targets makes production seamless.

Tips for Writing a Great YouTube Script

Writing for video is different from writing an essay or a blog post. Keep these tips in mind:

  • Write Conversationally: Use simple, natural sentences just like you are talking directly to a friend.
  • Account for B-Roll and Pauses: If your video includes clips, music intros, or demonstrations, you might need slightly fewer words because visual elements will fill the time.
