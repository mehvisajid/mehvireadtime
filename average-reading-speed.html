What is the Average Reading Speed of an Adult?

Quick Answer: The average reading speed for an adult is approximately 238 to 250 words per minute (WPM) when reading standard non-fiction material silently. For casual fiction books, it can increase to around 300 WPM.

Have you ever wondered how fast humans process written text? Whether you are trying to estimate how long a book will take to finish or preparing content for a blog, understanding reading speeds is extremely useful. While silent reading is quite fast, reading out loud (such as for speeches or presentations) drops significantly—as explored in our guide on how long a 5-minute speech takes.

Average Reading Speed Breakdown by Material Type

Different types of text require different levels of concentration, which directly changes your words-per-minute (WPM) rate:

Reading Material Average Speed (WPM) Comprehension Level
Technical / Academic Text 100 – 150 WPM High focus & detailed analysis
Standard Non-Fiction / Articles 238 – 250 WPM Standard comprehension
Fiction Novels / Stories 300 – 350 WPM Relaxed narrative flow
Skimming / Scanning 500+ WPM Surface-level keywords lookup

Factors That Affect How Fast We Read

No two readers are exactly alike. Several elements influence reading speeds on a daily basis:

  • Vocabulary and Familiarity: If you already know the subject terminology, your brain processes words much faster.
  • Formatting and Font Size: Clear layouts, proper spacing, and legible fonts prevent eye fatigue and boost reading speed.
  • Environment: Quiet spaces improve concentration compared to noisy or distracting surroundings.
