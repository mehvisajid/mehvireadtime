How Many Words is a 5-Minute Speech?

Quick Answer: A 5-minute speech is between 650 and 750 words when delivered at an average conversational speaking speed of 130 to 150 words per minute (WPM).

Whether you are preparing a school presentation, a wedding toast, or a business pitch, knowing the exact word count keeps you from rushing or running over your time limit. While silent reading is much faster—for instance, check out how long it takes to read 1,000 words silently—speaking aloud requires pauses for breathing and emphasis.

5-Minute Speech Word Count by Speaking Speed

Your exact word count depends on how fast you naturally speak and whether your topic requires dramatic pauses:

Speaking Pace Words Per Minute (WPM) 5-Minute Word Count Best Used For
Slow & Deliberate 100 – 120 WPM 500 – 600 words Keynotes, emotional stories, complex topics
Average / Conversational 130 – 150 WPM 650 – 750 words Class presentations, podcasts, business talks
Fast / Energetic 160 – 180 WPM 800 – 900 words Debates, radio announcements, auctioneers

How Many Pages is a 5-Minute Speech?

If you are writing your speech in Microsoft Word or Google Docs using standard formatting (12-point Arial or Times New Roman font with 1-inch margins):

  • Double-spaced: 650 to 750 words equals roughly 2.5 to 3 pages.
  • Single-spaced: 650 to 750 words equals roughly 1.3 to 1.5 pages.

3 Tips to Nail Your 5-Minute Speech Timing

  • Aim for 700 words: Writing around 700 words gives you a comfortable buffer for natural pauses and audience reactions.
  • Time yourself out loud: Reading in your head is always faster than speaking aloud. Practice with a timer at least twice.
  • Mark your pauses: Leave visual line breaks in your script where you want to pause for emphasis so you do not rush.
